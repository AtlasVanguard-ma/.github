# AtlasVanguard

AtlasVanguard is a financial observability and market intelligence initiative focused on derivatives analytics, systemic risk monitoring, and market structure research within Morocco’s evolving financial ecosystem.

## Focus Areas

* MASI & MASI20 market analytics
* Futures market observability
* Market stress indicators
* Volatility and correlation analysis
* Hedge and exposure analytics
* Systemic risk monitoring
* Macro-financial data integration

## Current Initiative

### MASI Futures Observatory

A platform designed to monitor and analyze the structural impact of derivatives and MASI Futures on the Moroccan financial market through:

* composite market stress indicators
* futures basis monitoring
* sector contagion analysis
* volatility regime detection
* institutional risk dashboards


## Mission

Build transparent and institutionally credible infrastructure for monitoring systemic risk and derivatives dynamics in Morocco’s financial markets.
